Substitutions Law III

  • Unlimited, at any stoppage, with your permission.
  • A cautioned player may be subbed at the time of the caution. The opponent may then sub one player.
  • Players enter at the halfway line only, after the outgoing player is fully off and you signal them on. Outgoing players may leave at the nearest boundary.
  • A sent-off player cannot be replaced. The team plays short for the rest of the game.
  • If a team drops below 5 players, the game cannot continue. One of the 5 must be the goalkeeper.
  • No guest players. A player can play for only one team in the tournament.

Uniforms and equipment Law IV

  • Home team is listed first on the schedule and wears dark. Visitors wear white or light.
  • Goalkeeper colors must differ from both teams and from you.
  • Shin guards required.
  • No earrings or jewelry. Taping or bandaging earrings is not allowed.
  • Religious medals and medical tags only if taped to the body.
  • Casts, splints and braces are your call: allowed if not dangerous or padded with soft material.

Field and sideline Law I

  • Both teams on the same side. Each team area starts 10 yards from halfway and runs 12 yards toward its goal.
  • Maximum 4 coaches or staff on the sideline. Each must be on the frozen cup roster or have a current PlayMetrics coaching pass.
  • Players and coaches stay in their team area, behind any spectator line or 5+ feet from the touchline. Exceptions: subs warming up, or staff you call on for an injured player.
  • Spectators on the opposite side, at least 5 feet from the touchlines and goal lines. Nobody behind the goals without Tournament Director approval. One warning, then they may be ejected.
  • Cameras like Veo on the spectator side, base at least 4 yards from the touchline.
  • No drones, horns, whistles, large megaphones or noisemakers.

Send-offs and suspensions Law XII

You report. Cup staff enforce.

Foul or abusive language, or persistent misconduct after a caution

Sits out the next tournament game.

Violent conduct

Sits out at least the next 2 tournament games.

Bench personnel dismissed

Sits out that team's next game, and no Cup role with any team until served.

Ejected coach or spectator

Must be 100 yards from the field within 5 minutes of notice. If not, abandon the match. Their team forfeits, 3–0 or the current score, whichever favors the opponent more.

  • Unserved suspensions carry to the player's next CSA league game or next Cup enrollment.
  • A dismissed coach or spectator may also cost the team 1 standings point. Cup staff decide.
  • If a team tells you about a suspended player or coach, record the name on your game report.
  • Harassment or insults toward referees or staff are not tolerated. Coaches are responsible for their supporters. Report it; the Tournament Committee and CSA handle discipline.

Weather and injuries

Siren

Play stops for rain, cold, high wind or lightning. When the siren sounds, clear the field. Play resumes on the next siren. Only the Tournament Director cancels, shortens or moves games.

Stopped games

If play doesn't resume and at least one half was completed, the score at the stoppage stands.

Suspected head injury

The on-site medical trainer evaluates and reports to Cup staff. The player cannot return until cleared by a medical professional.

After the game

Complete the game card in full

Final score, plus any suspended players or coaches who sat out if the team told you.

  • Tell CSA tournament staff about every send-off: players, coaches, spectators.
  • Submit a supplemental report for any send-off, abandonment or unusual incident.
  • No protests. Your on-field decisions are final and cannot be appealed.
  • Standings (3 for a win, 1 for a tie) and tiebreakers are handled by tournament staff, not you.
