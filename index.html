Lightning

Lightning within a 10 mile radius stops all games at that location with one long blast of a horn. Leave the fields immediately and wait in a vehicle or off site.

A full 30 minutes must pass with no strikes before the all clear. Every new strike resets the clock. Do not return until you hear the horn.

Delays

A game past halftime is called final after 60 minutes of delay.
