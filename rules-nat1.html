Kickoff and forfeit

A team needs 7 players ready within 10 minutes of scheduled kickoff. If not, it is a forfeit. No extra time in league play; a tie is a tie.

Your authority

Starts when you arrive at the site. Ends when you leave, or 30 minutes after the final whistle, whichever is first. Anything in that window is yours to deal with and report.

Substitutions 5.7

Moments, not numbers

Any number of players may come on at a stoppage with your permission. What is counted is the moment: each time a team substitutes is one moment, whatever the other team does.

  • Half-time substitutions do not count as a moment.
  • Coaches cannot agree between themselves to change this. If they try, it is still the Handbook.
  • A sent-off player is not replaced. The team plays short for the rest of the match.
  • The Colorado operator may publish its own substitution rule for this season. If CSA has issued one, that applies instead. Ask the assignor if unsure.

Kit and game day roster 6.1 · 5.3 · 6.5

  • Home team is listed first and wears light. Away wears dark. Every team must have both kits with them.
  • You decide what counts as light or dark. If there is a clash, the team causing it changes.
  • Every player needs a shirt number matching the game card. A temporary jersey is fine; note it on the card.
  • Goalkeeper distinguishable from every other player and from you.
  • Home team provides at least two game balls.
  • Glasses at your discretion. Casts only if you and the trainer (if there is one) approve.
Game day roster: maximum 18

You confirm eligibility before kickoff. Digital passcards are the norm; teams should have printed backups. No passcard, no play, unless the Commissioner has approved otherwise.

  • No more than four ECNL Regional League primary players in any match.
  • No more than two "trapped U15" players (8th graders) in a U14 match.
  • A player may play for only one team per day. A goalkeeper may appear on two game cards from the same club with the operator's written consent, and must play in goal both times.

Field and technical areas 6.6

  • Everybody at least 18 yards from the goal lines: coaches, players, spectators. Nobody behind the goals.
  • No coaching from within 18 yards of the goal line or from behind it, by anyone.
  • Coaches and players on the opposite side from spectators where the ground allows.
  • Coaches and spectators at least 2 yards from the touchline.
  • Nobody enters the field during the match unless you invite them.
Three officials expected

A licensed centre referee must be present or the match cannot go ahead. If ARs are short, you may appoint club assistants, limited to touchline and possession only.

Cards, send-offs and suspensions 5.15 · 5.16 · 5.17

No yellow card accumulation

Cautions do not carry over between matches in National 1.

Player sent off

Out for the rest of the day and the team's next National 1 match. Leaves the field of play. May stay in the bench area supervised by a team official only if no parent is available. Any further trouble and you may remove them from the whole field area.

Coach or team official sent off

Out of sight and hearing for the rest of the match. No contact with the team by any means, including phone or text. Rest of the day and the next match. If a suspended coach is present or communicating, the team forfeits.

Racist, discriminatory or spitting incidents, or a banned word

4 matches minimum, with review for more. The league keeps a banned-words list in the Handbook. Report it in full: exact words, who, when, who heard it. The U.S. Soccer Referee Abuse Policy applies to anything directed at you.

  • Cards from other competitions do not carry into National 1.
  • The operator reviews every send-off and may add a match. One added match cannot be appealed.
  • If a player or coach refuses to leave after being sent off, you may terminate. Forfeit 3–0 against them, or the actual score if they were already losing by more.

Spectators and trainers 7.1 · 5.13

  • The coach is responsible for their spectators. A coach can be sent off for what their sideline does.
  • You may ask a spectator to leave. If they refuse, you may terminate the match.
  • If a match is abandoned because of a team's coaches, players or spectators, that team forfeits.
Suspected head injury

If there is no certified athletic trainer at the ground, a player you suspect of a head injury does not return. Full stop. With a trainer present, the trainer decides. Either way, no return to any match without medical clearance.

Weather, protests, after the game 5.24 · 8.1 · 6.3

Weather

You may suspend or end a match for lightning, rain, fog or the like, before or during. Your decision is final. A match ended for weather counts if the first half is complete.

Protests

Your judgement cannot be protested. Only a misapplication of the rules can. Only the two teams may protest, in writing, through the operator.

After the whistle

Complete the game card. Report every send-off, every discriminatory incident, every temporary jersey. Post the score in the hub before you leave. The home team is fined if the score is not reported within 24 hours, and your report is what protects the referee crew if anything is questioned.
