Tied at full time

Group games: stays a tie. Semis, quarters, play-ins: straight to kicks from the mark, no extra time. Final only: extra time first, then kicks.

Added time

Do not add time for trifling stoppages. Keep the field on schedule. Add time for a serious injury.

Grace period

15 minutes from scheduled kickoff. A team not ready with the minimum players at the end of it forfeits. Forfeit is recorded 3–0.

Players and substitutions Laws II & III

AgeFormatMin to startBall
U9–U107v75not specified
U11–U129v96Size 4

  • Substitute at any stoppage, any number, with your permission.
  • A cautioned player may be substituted at the time of the caution. The other team may then substitute one player.
  • Subs enter at the halfway line only, and only after the player they replace is completely off and you have signalled. Players may leave at any point on the boundary.
  • A team may not replace a player sent off. They play short for the rest of the game.
  • One of the players on the field must be designated goalkeeper.
  • Each team supplies you with suitable balls. You choose which is used.

Equipment Law IV

  • Home team is listed first on the schedule and wears dark. Away wears white or light.
  • Goalkeeper distinguishable from both teams and from you.
  • No earrings of any kind. Not taped, not bandaged over. No jewellery.
  • Religious medals or medical tags are fine if taped to the body.
  • Splints, casts and braces at your judgement: not inherently dangerous, or padded with pliable material.
  • Shin guards required.

The field Laws I & X

9v911v11
Penalty mark8 yds12 yds
Free kick distance8 yds (U9–U12)10 yds (U13+)
  • Both teams on the same side. Each team area starts 10 yards from halfway and runs 12 yards toward the goal.
  • Spectators on the opposite side, at least 5 feet back from the line. Nobody behind the goals without the Tournament Director's permission. One warning, then ejection.
  • Cameras like Veo on the spectator side, base at least 4 yards from the touchline.
  • No drones, horns, whistles, megaphones or noisemakers.
  • Bench staff stay in the team area except to warm up a sub or when you call them on for an injured player.
  • Maximum 4 coaches or staff on the sideline.

Cards, sit-outs and dismissals Law XII

Send-off for foul or abusive language, or persistent misconduct after a caution

Sits out the next tournament game.

Send-off for violent conduct

Sits out a minimum of two tournament games.

Ejected coach, manager or spectator

Must be 100 yards from the field within 5 minutes of being told, or the match is abandoned. An abandoned game is a forfeit for their team.

  • Cards earned in Cup are served in Cup. They do not carry into league play.
  • Before kickoff the team representative must tell you the name of anyone sitting out a suspension. Record that name on your game report.
  • Dismissed bench staff also sit out and cannot take part in any other club's Cup game until served.
  • Harassment of any official by a player, coach or spectator is a disciplinary matter for CSA. Team representatives are responsible for their supporters.
  • Severe misconduct: the Site Director can convene a hearing. Send a full report.

After the game

Complete the game card in detail.

Tell CSA tournament staff about anyone ejected. File a supplemental report for anything unusual. Post the score in the hub before you leave the field.

Protests

A referee's judgement cannot be protested, only a misapplication of the Laws. A protest is lodged verbally with you and the opposing coach immediately after the game, then in writing with a $200 fee within two hours. Video is not considered.

Weather

A siren clears the fields. Everyone to their cars. The siren again means play resumes. A game stopped after one full half stands at the score if it is not restarted.

Suspected concussion

The on-site trainer evaluates. If a head injury is suspected the player is done for the day and cannot play again in league or Cup until cleared in writing and CSA has notified the club. USYS concussion protocol applies.
