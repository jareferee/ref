U11 — no heading Deliberate heading is not permitted in U11 flights, per US Soccer. U11 and U12 play 9v9 including the goalkeeper. Some U12 teams opted up and play 11v11 in the U13 bracket.

Substitutions

Not free substitution. Restarts where a substitution may be made:

  • Prior to a throw-in in your favour
  • Prior to a goal kick, either team
  • Following a goal, either team
  • Following an injury to a player from either team
  • At half time
  • On the opposing team's throw-in, but only if that team also has a player at the halfway line

All substitution opportunities are at the discretion of the referee in charge.

Equipment

  • Shin guards required. Socks, matching colour, pulled over them.
  • Hard casts are not allowed. Wrapping a cast in foam or bubble wrap does not make it legal. Soft casts and braces need a waiver from the Tournament Director.
  • No earrings of any kind. Taping over earrings or any dangerous accessory is not permitted. This includes hair control devices with hard parts.
  • Matching jerseys and shorts. Jerseys tucked in, numbered, no two players the same number.

Home team is listed first, supplies the match ball, and wears light or white. Away wears dark. On a colour conflict, the home team changes.

Teams on the same side of the field. Spectators opposite. Teams report to the field 15 minutes before kickoff.

Send-offs and suspensions

All red and yellow cards are reported to the Tournament Director. Minimum suspension by send-off code:

2CT1 gameSecond caution in the same game
DGF1 gameDenying an obvious goal-scoring opportunity by a foul
DGH1 gameDenying a goal or opportunity by deliberately handling
AL2 gamesOffensive, insulting or abusive language or gestures
SFP2 gamesSerious foul play
VCRest of eventViolent conduct
SRest of eventSpitting at an opponent or any other person

These are minimums. The discipline committee may suspend for longer, and the Tournament Director has final say on any suspension. All red cards are reported to the state association of the player's team.

File every send-off through the hub so the sit-out list is accurate for the next day.

Harassment and game termination

Harassment of officials or players will not be tolerated. You may suspend the game if you judge it necessary. Where it comes from spectators, remind the coaches that they are responsible for the behaviour of their parents and supporters. If it cannot be stopped, you have the authority to terminate.

The result of a terminated game is decided by the Tournament Director — a winner declared, a forfeit, or a full replay.

Weather

Before kickoff the Tournament Director decides whether a game is played. Once it has started the decision to continue rests with the referee.

If you suspend for weather, the tournament committee decides when it restarts. A second stoppage terminates the game automatically.

A terminated game counts as complete if one half has elapsed — the score at stoppage stands. Terminated before half, it restarts at the point of delay with the same score, at the Tournament Director's discretion.

Scoring, forfeits and protests

3 win   1 draw   0 loss

A forfeit is recorded 5–0 to the opposing team, worth 3 points. Same for a team caught playing an ineligible player on a second team.

Semi-finals and finals go straight to kicks from the penalty mark if level at the end of regulation. No extra time.

All referee decisions are final. No protests are allowed except on player eligibility or an incorrectly reported score.

Rosters

Maximum 22 players for U13–U19, 16 for U11 and U12. Up to 4 guest players and up to 6 club pass players. All players need a current, valid USYSA or US Club pass.

No roster changes after registration. A player may play for only one team in the tournament.
